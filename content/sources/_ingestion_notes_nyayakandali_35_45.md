extracted by an AI agent from the OCR text layer of the Jha printed edition

## Phase / pipeline status

- `sa_devanagari` and `sa_iast` are **both empty strings** for every record
  in this pass, per the agent brief. Phase-B printed-edition pass (and/or
  GRETIL-derived Devanagari for Praśastapāda where applicable) is the
  proper path to populate these fields. The schema now allows empty
  Devanagari (`src/lib/schema.ts`: `sa_devanagari: z.string()`).
- `literal_en` is **Jha verbatim from the local PDF**. Page-number
  bleed-in (e.g. `Page 50.]`, running headers like
  `PRA^ASTAPÄDA BHÄSHYA—CHAP. IV. SECTION (3).`) and characteristic OCR
  garbles (`Akäca` for *Ākāśa*, `Atmä` for *Ātmā*, `Saroaväya` for
  *Samavāya*, `ttye`/`teas`/`uts` for ordinary English words, the U+000C
  form-feed page-break, etc.) are preserved as the agent brief requires.
  No silent correction was applied.
- `ml_draft.text` — manipravāḷam-conscious Malayalam draft, generated by
  this agent. Sanskrit termini are kept in Sanskrit (Malayalam script) per
  the glossary; Malayalam prose surrounds them. Faithful to the structure
  of the commentary, not summary-only. For the very long Text 44
  commentary (`Ātmā` section, ~88 K characters), the draft renders the
  major argumentative architecture but does not attempt line-by-line
  expansion — flagged as a *draft outline* in the body and to be expanded
  by a śāstrika reviewer.
- `en_simple_draft.text` — accessible English alongside Jha, never
  replacing him; *termini technici* are kept as italicised Sanskrit with
  plain-English gloss in surrounding prose.

## Śāstrika concerns flagged for review

1. **Text 44 — *Ātmā* — is exceptionally long.** The Kandalī's *ātma-vāda*
   here is effectively a darshana-by-darshana refutation (Cārvāka,
   Bauddha, Prābhākara, Bhāṭṭa, Sāṃkhya, Yoga). The current drafts
   summarise the argument-structure but cannot do full justice to the
   close exegesis without a śāstrika pass; the draft itself flags this.
2. **OCR running headers embedded mid-paragraph.** `literal_en` for Texts
   42, 43, 44 in particular contains form-feed characters (U+000C) and
   page-running-header bleed-in like
   `PRA^ASTAPÄDA BHÄSHYA—CHAP. IV. SECTION (8).`. These are *not*
   Śrīdhara's words; they are OCR artefacts. They are preserved here per
   the agent brief, but a Phase-B printed-edition transcription will
   strip them.
3. **Sanskrit text deferred entirely.** The brief constrained
   `sa_devanagari` and `sa_iast` to empty strings; the Sanskrit will be
   added in Phase B from the printed edition (and/or a Devanagari-OCR
   second pass per `NOTICE.md`).
4. **Text 38 (Fire) embeds a Western-history NOTE.** The OCR text after
   the Kandalī's *yugapad-saṃyoga* discussion contains a paragraph
   beginning `NOT«.—Ever since Vai9§suika philosophy became known to the
   western world…`. This is an editorial footnote by Jha, not Śrīdhara's
   commentary. It is preserved in `literal_en` (it sits inside the
   Commentary block in the OCR), but a śāstrika may wish to split it out
   as a separate annotation.
5. **Section boundaries between Texts 36 and 37.** The printed edition
   inserts a `NOTES:—` paragraph just before `SECTION (2). OF WATER`
   (lines 3191–3203). This block is mostly Jha's editorial notes on
   *pṛthivītvābhisambandhāt* and *pākaja*; it is preserved at the end of
   `nk.visesha-dravya.36`'s `literal_en` block. Phase-B cleanup may want
   to relocate it.
